Who we are

Grassroots to first team, one pathway.

Founded in KwaDukuza, Ilembe FDA exists to give young players in the region a real, structured route into professional football — with the coaching, exposure and character development to get there.

Our Mission

To identify, develop and place talented players from the Ilembe district into professional and semi-professional football, while instilling discipline, education and community values at every age group.

What we offer

  • Structured training for every age group, U12 through to the Senior team
  • Qualified coaching staff and a shared playing philosophy across all squads
  • Match exposure through local leagues, tournaments and friendlies
  • Player welfare support alongside schooling and academics
  • A clear pathway from youth development into the first team

How registration works

Fill in the form and one of our coaches will contact you to arrange a trial session at your age group's training venue.

Bring your own boots and shin guards. Trials are free — no payment is ever required to be assessed by Ilembe FDA.

Parents/guardians of players under 18 must complete the form on the player's behalf.
